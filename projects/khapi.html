Khapi.

Khapi is a rebranding project for an existing specialty coffee shop in Barcelona. After reviewing its previous identity and understanding the client’s vision, I developed a new visual direction inspired by Mediterranean culture and the everyday ritual of coffee. The project includes a brand identity, a brand book and an initial system of social media posts and stories.

01 / The identity

The brand book.

A visual identity built around an expressive character, classical typography and Mediterranean illustrations. The brand book brings these elements together and defines how to use them consistently.

02 / The visual system

Bringing the brand to the feed.

A social media system designed to put the product at the heart of the brand’s communication.

The aim was to highlight the coffee, food and experience through warm photography and clear, concise messages. Close-up images draw attention to texture and detail, while photographs of the space communicate the welcoming atmosphere behind the brand.

The feed alternates product photography with typographic compositions and illustrated pieces. The brand’s blue and ivory palette, expressive serif typography and Mediterranean motifs connect the posts without making every layout look the same.

Stories extend this system into a vertical format, creating space for everyday moments, product highlights and simple explanations of the coffee-making process. Together, the initial posts and stories establish a coherent foundation for future content.
