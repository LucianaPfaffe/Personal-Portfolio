Alma Marina.

Alma Marina is a Reiki and holistic therapy practice focused on well-being and personal connection. I developed its visual identity to reflect a sense of calm, balance and warmth.

02 / Creative process

Finding the
balance.

The identity brings together expressive serif typography, a compact monogram and a soft palette of greens, lilac and warm neutrals.

These elements create a balance between personality and simplicity. The monogram also becomes a repeating pattern, giving the brand a recognizable detail that can carry through printed materials and everyday applications.
